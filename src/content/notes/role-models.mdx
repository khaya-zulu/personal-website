I spend a lot of time (more than I should) looking at the profiles of software engineers.

After two years of saving engineers in a notes app, I built [My Dev List](https://mydevlist.dev), a directory for the profiles I kept reopening.

I add someone when I catch myself thinking: "There's knowledge, experience or interests that this person has that would be cool to have right now."

**A few examples:**

- [**Matt Pocock**](https://github.com/mattpocock), a great engineer and educator who's transferred all that knowledge into AI engineering, in a way that's not just a pundit or hype, but an engineer using the tools the way an engineer would.
- [**Evan Bacon**](https://evanbacon.dev), his mastery of React Native, open source and personal community building is a great example of how focus and passion make up a world-class product engineer.

![Twitter post by expo](/notes/role-models/twitter.png)

- [**Kelsey Hightower**](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/kubernetes-and-retiring-at-the-top), one of my favourite technical communicators, he has shaped my importance of being able to communicate ideas well.
- [**Daniel Hollick**](https://alcohollick.com), South African-born design engineer at Cursor. Someone from home building beautiful software (publicly) is a reminder of what's possible from here.

> - [2 hour pragmatic engineer podcast episode with Kelsey Hightower](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UlXpOGIpITM)
> - [Making Software by Daniel Hollick](https://www.makingsoftware.com/)

It takes a good set of examples to set the bar for what great looks like. For me that's my [My Dev List](https://mydevlist.dev).
